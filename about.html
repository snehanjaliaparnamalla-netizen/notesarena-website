About JEE Compass

Every student's preparation deserves a clearer direction.

Our Story

JEE Compass began as an idea during my own JEE preparation. As a student, I realised how useful it would be to have chapter-wise notes, important formulas and revision resources organised in one place.

Why We Started

Preparing for JEE involves understanding numerous concepts, revising regularly and managing study materials. Finding clear and well-organised resources can sometimes be challenging. JEE Compass aims to make this journey a little simpler by bringing useful study materials together.

About the Creator

JEE Compass is a student-created project built by a Class 11 JEE aspirant who understands the challenges of competitive exam preparation. This project is also an opportunity to use technology to create something helpful for fellow students.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make organised learning resources more accessible to JEE aspirants and help students study, practise and revise with greater clarity.
